Hierarchical Model with Light: Merce Cunningham Loops data

Motion capture data of Merce Cunningham performing Loops, his solo dance for the hands, recorded August 23, 2000 and released by the OpenEnded Group (openendedgroup.com).
Motion capture data: Courtesy OpenEnded Group and Cunningham Foundation, licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 US.

Edit vertex and fragment shaders on the left to get the result instantly.
Use mouse to drag and rotate.
Use arrow keys and w, s keys to move the camera.
